In my iOS 13 Safari test environment, using 3D Touch and then downloading an image caused the webcam stream to fail. This is a record of that observed environment; it does not establish that every AppleWebKit 605 or later version has the same bug.

I don't know why but after using 3Dtouch and downloadimage will cause webcam error.

***Re-initializing the webcam stream worked in my test.***
