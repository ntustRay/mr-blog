## Principles

- **34 topics total:** 32 new Reviews + 2 updates of recent reviews (05 and 06).
- Existing 2026 reviews retain URL and publication date; add an updatedDate and join the series.
- Legacy posts are historical records; new reviews do not silently rewrite history.
- Each Review contains a GitHub Stars snapshot for genuinely comparable public source repositories, Pros / Cons, trade-offs, use cases and a validation plan.
- Stars are not quality or performance rankings. Commercial products, browser standards and conceptual comparisons may have no comparable Stars.
- Public posts may cite only public repositories and public product behavior; no private source, credentials, infrastructure details, or internal metrics.
- Do not imply that reviewed alternatives were all used by the author. Separate tested, observed, documented and hypothetical claims.
